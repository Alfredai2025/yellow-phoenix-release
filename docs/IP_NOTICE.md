**Implications:**

  a registered senior mark in class 042 exists.
- **To-do before filing**: formal TESS/TMsearch clearance, conflict opinion
  from a trademark attorney, and consideration of the full composite mark
  (word + phoenix device).

## 3. Trademark vs. copyright — the important distinction

The AGPL-3.0 grants broad rights in the *code*. It grants **no rights in the
name or logo**. Third parties may fork the code under AGPL but may not use
"Yellow Phoenix" branding in a way that suggests endorsement by the project.
Use of the name for truthful reference ("compatible with Yellow Phoenix",
"forked from Yellow Phoenix") is tolerated and standard; merchandise,
product branding, or confusingly similar marks are not granted.

## 5. Reporting IP infringement

**If you believe content in this repository infringes your copyright:**
1. Open an issue at
   https://github.com/Alfredai2025/yellow-phoenix-release/issues
   with: identification of the work, the URL of the allegedly infringing
   material, your contact information, and a good-faith statement that the
   use is unauthorized.
2. The maintainers will respond promptly, typically by removing or replacing
   the challenged material pending review, in the spirit of DMCA §512
   notice-and-takedown. Counter-notices may be filed via the same channel.

**If you believe the project infringes your trademark or patent:**
contact via the same issue tracker with specifics; the project prefers
correction, attribution, or rename over dispute.

**If you are a contributor and believe your contribution was used outside the
license:** the AGPL is enforced through its own terms; raise it via the issue
tracker or the maintainer's GitHub contact.
